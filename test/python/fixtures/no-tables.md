# Methods

## Study site and sampling

We sampled twelve grassland plots across the study catchment between March 2021
and September 2023. Each plot was 5 m by 5 m, and the twelve were stratified by
soil depth into three bands of four.

Cover was recorded from nadir photographs at 300 dpi and scored with a point
grid of 100 intersections per frame. Root biomass came from two 5 cm cores per
plot per season, oven-dried at 60 °C to constant mass.

## Analysis

Recovery rate was the ratio of post-drought to pre-drought cover, and we
compared the fertilised and control groups with a linear mixed model with plot
as a random effect. All analyses ran in Python 3.11.
