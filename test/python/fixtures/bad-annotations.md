# Nitrogen supply controls grassland recovery after drought

## Results

Grasslands recovered faster where nitrogen was {==abundant==}. That abundance
lifted the recovery rate {--by a factor of 2.8--} in the fertilised plots
(p = 0.002, n = 48). That 2.8-fold advantage persisted through the second
growing season, when fertilised swards regained {~~91%~>a large fraction~~} of
pre-drought cover against 54% in the controls (Figure 2a).
